Dubai Onion Export Process – Complete A to Z Guide

Exporting onions from India to Dubai (UAE) is a high-demand, repeat-order and profitable export business. With proper quality control, packing standards and secure payment terms, this business can be scaled long term.

🌍 Why Indian onions are in demand in Dubai?

  • Large Indian population & restaurants
  • Strong hotel & catering industry
  • Year-round bulk buying

🧅 Preferred onion specifications

  • Indian Red Onion
  • Size 50–70 mm
  • Dry skin, moisture free

📄 Required export documents

  • IEC Code
  • APEDA Registration
  • GST
  • Phytosanitary Certificate
