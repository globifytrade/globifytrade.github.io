How to Start an Export Business from India
भारतामधून Export Business कसा सुरू करावा

1. Export Business म्हणजे काय?

मराठी:
Export Business म्हणजे भारतात तयार होणारी उत्पादने परदेशातील खरेदीदारांना विकण्याचा व्यवसाय. भारत हा कृषी उत्पादने, मसाले, धान्य, औषधे, टेक्सटाईल आणि इंजिनिअरिंग वस्तूंसाठी जगभर प्रसिद्ध आहे. योग्य नियोजन, documentation आणि market research असल्यास export business कमी गुंतवणुकीत सुरू करता येतो. Dollar, Dirham सारख्या foreign currency मध्ये payment मिळाल्यामुळे profit margin जास्त असतो.

English:
An export business involves selling products manufactured or sourced in India to international buyers. India is globally known for agricultural products, spices, grains, pharmaceuticals, textiles, and engineering goods. With proper planning, documentation, and market research, export business can be started with low investment and offers higher profit margins due to foreign currency earnings.

2. Market Research आणि Product Selection

मराठी:
योग्य product निवडणे हा export business मधील सर्वात महत्वाचा टप्पा आहे. Google Trends, Import Export Data, Trade Map आणि B2B portals वापरून कोणत्या देशात कोणत्या उत्पादनाची मागणी आहे हे शोधा. High demand आणि low competition असलेले product निवडल्यास success chance वाढतो.

English:
Product selection is the most critical step in export business. Use Google Trends, Import Export data, Trade Map, and B2B portals to identify product demand in different countries. Choosing high-demand and low-competition products increases success chances.

3. Legal Registration & Licenses

मराठी:
Export सुरू करण्यासाठी Import Export Code (IEC) आवश्यक आहे. त्यासोबत GST Registration, Current Account, MSME Registration घ्यावे लागते. काही उत्पादने जसे मसाले, अन्नपदार्थ, औषधे यासाठी Spices Board, FSSAI, APEDA registration आवश्यक असते.

English:
To start exporting, Import Export Code (IEC) is mandatory. GST registration, current account, and MSME registration are also required. Certain products such as spices, food, and pharmaceuticals require additional registrations like Spices Board, FSSAI, and APEDA.

4. Buyer Finding & International Marketing

मराठी:
Buyer मिळवणे हा export business चा core भाग आहे. Alibaba, IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Global Sources यांसारख्या B2B portals वर professional profile तयार करा. LinkedIn वर importers आणि distributors शी direct संपर्क साधा. Trade fairs आणि exhibitions मध्ये face-to-face trust build होतो.

English:
Finding buyers is the core of export business. Create professional profiles on B2B portals like Alibaba, IndiaMART, TradeIndia, and Global Sources. Use LinkedIn to connect directly with importers and distributors. Trade fairs help in building face-to-face trust.

5. Pricing Strategy & Cost Calculation

मराठी:
Export pricing करताना product cost, packaging, transport, customs, freight, insurance आणि bank charges लक्षात घ्याव्या लागतात. Incoterms (FOB, CIF) नीट समजून pricing ठरवा.

English:
Export pricing should include product cost, packaging, transportation, customs, freight, insurance, and banking charges. Understand Incoterms like FOB and CIF before finalizing pricing.

6. Export Documentation Process

मराठी:
Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Shipping Bill, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin हे मुख्य documents आहेत. Documentation मध्ये चूक झाल्यास shipment customs मध्ये अडकू शकते.

English:
Key export documents include Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Shipping Bill, Bill of Lading, and Certificate of Origin. Errors in documentation can delay shipments at customs.

7. Logistics & Shipping Management

मराठी:
Sea freight आणि Air freight हे दोन shipping modes आहेत. योग्य freight forwarder निवडल्यास वेळ आणि खर्च वाचतो.

English:
Sea freight and air freight are the two main shipping modes. Choosing the right freight forwarder helps save time and cost.

8. Export Payment Methods & Risk Management

मराठी:
Advance payment आणि Letter of Credit हे सुरक्षित payment methods आहेत. ECGC insurance वापरून payment risk कमी करता येतो.

English:
Advance payment and Letter of Credit are secure payment methods. ECGC insurance helps reduce payment risks.

9. Government Schemes & Export Incentives

मराठी:
RoDTEP, GST refund, Duty Drawback यांसारख्या government schemes मुळे export profit वाढतो.

English:
Government schemes like RoDTEP, GST refund, and Duty Drawback increase export profitability.

10. Common Mistakes & Long-Term Growth

मराठी:
Buyer verification न करणे, चुकीची pricing, documentation mistakes या common चुका आहेत. Long-term growth साठी quality आणि relationship building महत्वाचे आहे.

English:
Common mistakes include poor buyer verification, wrong pricing, and documentation errors. Long-term growth depends on quality and strong relationships.
